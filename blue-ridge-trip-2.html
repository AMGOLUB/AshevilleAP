Before we go

  • Buy tubing tickets online and sign both waivers before Saturday
  • Confirm the reservation at Plant, Asheville
  • Check the weather for Aug 22, 2026, and radar before the summit
  • Download offline maps, no signal past Bull Gap or in Pisgah

The summit runs 15 to 20 degrees cooler than town and the wind is honest up there. Bring the jackets even though it is August.
